# Decisions

One line each: date, decision, reason.

- 2026-10-04: Head and torso are one rigid body with two colliders. The plan says "rigid head and torso", and a joint between them adds nothing.
- 2026-10-04: All joints use Rapier's ForceBased motor model (stiffness in N*m per radian). The default model was far too weak to hold an arm up, measured in a headless test.
- 2026-10-04: Elbow and wrist are stiff; the shoulder torque cap (`arm.shoulderMaxTorque`) sets how hard a swing is. A soft elbow made the stick whip and lag about 30 frames behind the aim.
- 2026-10-04: Damage is measured every frame for weapon colliders touching another fighter (closing speed along the contact normal x reduced mass x weapon multiplier), not only at first contact, so a stick already resting on a body can still hit when swung.
- 2026-10-04: Tuning hot reload rebuilds the world (via `Sim.reset()`), because body sizes, masses and joint limits are baked in at creation. Per-frame numbers (forces, thresholds) update live in place.
- 2026-10-04: Placeholder sounds are synthesised with WebAudio oscillators, so there are no audio files or licences yet. Howler.js (named in the plan) arrives with real licensed SFX in the art and audio phase.
- 2026-10-04: The training dummy is a fighter built by the same code with no input and no stick, so it balances, takes damage and dies like a player does.
- 2026-10-04: Phase 0 "deploy to a public URL" is not done yet. It needs a Cloudflare Pages or Netlify account, which is the owner's call.
- 2026-10-04 [superseded, see the charge/throw entries below]: Added a dedicated cock-back button (hold right-click or left trigger/bumper): the weapon arm pulls up and behind, and on release gets a torque burst that grows with hold time (up to 0.5 s). Chosen by the owner (Spiderheck-style). It replaces the old "hold click for power" boost.
- 2026-10-04 [superseded]: A plain click is the same mechanism on a 5-frame automatic timer: a quick chop with the stick, a jab when unarmed. Every punch now starts with a pull-back, which fixes point-blank punches doing nothing.
- 2026-10-04 [superseded]: The release burst scales shoulder spring stiffness as well as the torque cap. Raising the cap alone changed nothing (measured): swing speed was limited by the spring, not the cap.
- 2026-10-04: Damage curve lowered (damageScale 5 -> 1.5) so a plain swing is a medium hit and a charged one is heavy. At the old value every swing hit the 60 damage cap and the cock bonus was invisible.
- 2026-10-04: impactMin stays at 10: walking into the dummy with a stick no longer hurts it (measured 0 damage).
- 2026-10-04: `?stress` URL option adds two scripted flailing fighters for a 4-fighter frame-time check. They are not AI (bots are out of scope).
- 2026-10-04: One arm per fighter (owner's call: less confusing controls). The off-hand is removed entirely, so a fighter is 4 physics parts (torso+head, upper arm, forearm, stick) and unarmed punches no longer alternate hands. Fewer bodies also helps the frame budget.
- 2026-10-04 [superseded]: With one arm, a full cock swing got much stronger and tipped the fighter over (tilt 1.2 rad), so cock.releaseMul went 4 -> 2.5 (measured: click impact ~20, half charge ~26, full charge ~59, tilt 0.64).
- 2026-10-04: Shields are a weapon you hold instead of a sword (owner chose this with the one-arm change), so block is the shield's swing. Revisit in Phase 4 when weapon data files are written.
- 2026-10-04: Style test is built as a drop-in slot: `public/art/test_bg.webp` (or png/jpg) shows behind the arena if present; grain and vignette are generated in code. The painted image itself comes from the owner's AI tool, so the test is not finished until they supply it.
- 2026-10-04: Removed the canvas grain overlay (owner: "weird"). The vignette stays; set finish.vignetteAlpha to 0 to remove it.
- 2026-10-04: Controls remapped by the owner. Left-click (hold) = charge: the weapon stays on the aim (no front or back wind-up pose) and loads momentum; release lunges the fighter along the aim with a torque burst. Right-click = throw: the arm cocks back automatically (8 frames), then the weapon flies along the aim. The instant "click" attack was removed.
- 2026-10-04: Damage is hit speed x the weapon's own factor, nothing else: impact = closing speed (m/s) x weapon.impactFactor (stick 2.2, fists 1.6), then damage = (impact - impactMin) x damageScale, capped. The old reduced-mass term was dropped (the victim's mass is constant, so it only added a hidden fudge factor). The lunge and throw speeds count automatically because closing speed includes the body's and weapon's own motion.
- 2026-10-04: "Swing disconnected from the mouse" had two causes, both measured. (1) The shoulder spring was badly underdamped: it overshot the aim by 1.2 rad and rang. (2) Rapier's default 4 solver iterations made the 3-body arm chain soft, which is why torque caps, stiffness and damping changes did nothing. Fix: the shoulder is now a velocity follower (turn rate = shoulderTrack x error, plus the mouse's own turn rate as feed-forward) and the solver runs 32 iterations / 4 PGS (sim.solverIterations, sim.pgsIterations; cost is about 0.09 ms for 4 fighters). Real-mouse sweep error went from about 1 rad to 0.17 rad.
- 2026-10-04: Power limits so flicks and throws cannot flip the fighter: shoulderMaxRate 14 rad/s, shoulderMaxTorque 300, balance kp 800 / kd 60 / max 600. At the earlier values (40 rad/s, 600) a plain mouse flick tilted the fighter 2.7 rad (upside down).
- 2026-10-04: The walking controller is disabled during the lunge burst (it braked the fighter to zero and cancelled the lunge). Lunge impulse 100 gives about 10 m/s and a 2 m slide at full charge.
- 2026-10-04: A thrown weapon is a normal attacker (stick part with its own velocity), so its damage follows the same speed x factor rule. A stick lost off the edge still returns to the owner's hand.
- 2026-10-04: Fixed fast key taps (R, F3) being missed: keyup removed the key before the game loop checked it. Taps are now recorded separately.
- 2026-10-04: Shapes are drawn 100x larger and scaled down so Pixi gives round heads instead of octagons.
- 2026-10-04: "Long melee" club hold (owner request; Q2b, Q3b, Q4b answered, Q1 taken as recommended). The club is no longer collinear with the arm. The arm is posed with a bent elbow and a wrist angle (tuning.longMelee): guard = club upright in front, charge = club raised above the head leaning slightly back, slam = arm straightens and swings down through the aim. Poses are written for a fighter facing right and mirrored by the aim side (with hysteresis near vertical). The forearm still follows the cursor; elbow and wrist are springs, so the club head lags and whips when you flick the mouse.
- 2026-10-04: After a charge release the club stays raised for slamDelay frames while the fighter flies forward, then comes down. Without the delay the slam finished about 10 frames before the lunge arrived, so contact speed was just the lunge speed. The release distance is now the skill: measured best slam range 2.5-3.2 m, mistimed slams do about 20.
- 2026-10-04: The lunge follows the aim but is limited to 35 degrees of horizontal (charge.lungeMaxAngle) so it throws you at the opponent, not into the floor or the sky (owner Q3b).
- 2026-10-04: Damage curve is now convex: damage = damageScale x (impact - impactMin)^damageExp, capped at damageMax = 100 (a full HP bar). Measured: gentle swing 0, plain mouse flick 0-20, well-timed full slam 85-100 (a one-shot), mistimed slam about 20 (owner Q4b: a max-charge slam can one-shot, plain swings take several hits).
- 2026-10-04: burstRateShare (0.5) limits how much of the burst's extra strength raises the arm's turn-rate cap, so the slam stays a smooth arc and not a snap (full-charge head speed about 38 m/s peak).
- 2026-10-04: Dev aid: open the game with ?slow=0.15 to run at 15% speed and study the slam.
- 2026-10-04: Owner feedback batch. Knockback cut (knockbackScale 1.5 -> 0.9, max 90 -> 45). The random spin on every hit (a random torque of up to about 36 rad/s) was the "torso flipping separate from the body" the owner saw. It is replaced by a small backward tip of the victim away from the blow (spinScale 0.04). The killing blow still launches harder (killLaunchMul 1.3).
- 2026-10-04: Hits to the head do 1.6x damage (combat.headMult). The head is its own collider on the torso body; a hit there flags the event so the screen shake, splat and sound are bigger.
- 2026-10-04: E (grab/drop) and throwing removed entirely (owner). The club is now permanently in the hand, so unarmed play exists only via fighter.startArmed = false (for testing the punch) or later disarm mechanics. Right-click is unassigned until the owner decides what "hit" means (a timing-release technique input; asked as a grilling round).
- 2026-10-04: Unarmed left-click is a real punch with three phases: wind-up (upper arm back, elbow folded, body leans back), throw (arm whips straight out along the aim, body lunges in), recover. Poses live in tuning.punch and are built to be reused by other short melee weapons later.
- 2026-10-04: Whole-body pushes. Jumps, lunges, walking and knockback now give every part of the fighter (arm, club) the same change in speed. Before, only the torso moved and the arm and club dragged it back: the jump reached only 1.26 m of the intended height, and hits felt rubbery. Jump speed 8.5 now gives 1.7 m (measured); it was 10.5 (2.5 m) after the fix, which the owner found far too high.
- 2026-10-04: Lean: the body leans into its motion by speed (0.06 per m/s) plus the gap between wanted and actual speed (0.1 per m/s), so it leans about 35 degrees when running, skids back about 40 degrees on reversing, winds back before a lunge or punch, and throws forward on a slam. The walking acceleration was lowered to 26 for a looser, more sliding feel.
- 2026-10-04: Legs. First attempt (two-bone IK with a walk cycle) looked mechanical and uncanny to the owner. Replaced with simple one-piece stick legs that are drawn but run on their own spring physics (pendulums hung from the hip: they swing with a gentle run target, lag when the body accelerates, and flop in the air), in the spirit of Stick Fight. The physical body is still one capsule, so this costs nothing in the simulation. Truly physical legs (the body standing on them) were NOT done: that is the plan's biggest tuning risk. Asked the owner whether they want it.
- 2026-10-04: Death highlight. On a kill: head comes off onto a floppy neck and two real physics legs appear on loose hips (a real ragdoll), extra launch, a longer freeze (killHitStop 10 frames), a big screen kick, a burst of paint splashes, a boom sound, and about 0.9 s of quarter-speed slow motion (done in the game loop, so the simulation stays deterministic).
- 2026-10-04: Dodge (owner request for later gameplay; built minimally now). Shift (or B / left bumper on a pad): for 0.6 s the fighter is on a "background plane": it collides only with the ground, so fighters and weapons pass straight through, it cannot hit anyone, and it cannot be hit. 6 s cooldown. It stays on the background plane until nobody is standing inside it, so nobody is flung apart when it ends. For now the look is a stand-in for the later 2.5D depth: smaller and ghosted. Projectiles do not exist yet; they will use the same collision rule.
- 2026-10-04: Jump and dodge keys count a very fast tap (a key released before the game looked), same fix as R and F3 earlier.
- 2026-10-04: Mistake caught and fixed: a quick edit changed the club's length from 1.1 to 0.43 because two settings were both called "length". Restored. (Tests did not catch it; worth remembering that settings with the same name in different groups need a careful edit.)
- 2026-10-04: Facing lock (owner bug report: holding left-click while facing right made the swing turn around). The facing direction now follows the cursor only when no attack is under way; it is locked from the press of the attack button until the lunge or punch is over, and the lunge always goes the way the fighter faces, even if the cursor is behind. A test found a second bug in the same area: on the frame the button was released the lock was off, so the fighter flipped and lunged backward; fixed by also locking while the charge counter is running.
- 2026-10-04: Death is calm (owner: the slow motion, shake and the rest were too much for four players). Removed the kill slow motion, the extra screen kick, the paint burst, the boom, the longer freeze and the extra launch. What stays: the ragdoll (head on a floppy neck, loose legs, body keeps the speed of the hit), the fighter greying out so the body reads as dead, and a short comic sound. Normal hits still shake the screen a little (shake settings), to be reviewed.
- 2026-10-04: Right-click is drop and pick up (owner). With a club in hand it lets go: the club keeps the speed of the swing plus a small push (drop.push), and as a thrown club it does damage by the same speed x weapon rule as any hit. With empty hands it picks the club up if it is within drop.pickupRange (1.6 m) of the body, and not for the first 40 frames after dropping it. The walk-over pickup I had built first was replaced by this. Only a fighter's own club can be picked up for now; picking up other weapons comes with the arena weapon pickups. A club lost in the void stays lost until respawn (the fighter can punch).
- 2026-10-04: A dropped club is no longer part of the body: it is left out of the body mass and out of jumps, lunges and knockback pushes.
- 2026-10-04: Dodge look redone (owner: no obvious shrinking). The fighter turns toward the screen (drawn narrower, dodge.visualSquash), is drawn in a layer behind every other fighter, a little darker and a touch higher, then turns back; on the way out it faces whichever way the cursor says. Collisions are unchanged: it only touches the ground. Checked in the live game: the dodger walks through the dummy, is hidden behind it, and comes out the far side.
- 2026-10-04: Screen shake only on big hits (owner): hits below shake.minImpact (45) do not shake at all; above it the shake grows with the hit, capped at shake.max (16 px). The grey tint on dead fighters was removed (owner): death visuals wait for the art phase; for now the ragdoll and knockback carry it.
- 2026-10-04: Dodge while charging is allowed and cancels the charge: the charge, the lunge burst and any punch wind-up are dropped, and the attack button is locked until it is let go (chargeLocked), so a new charge needs a fresh press.
- 2026-10-04: Unarmed punch can be charged. Hold left-click to wind up (the fist is drawn back further and the body leans back more the longer you hold, up to 0.5 s); let go to throw. Even a tap draws back for the minimum wind-up (6 frames) and throws a quick punch at 45% power. Power scales the lunge, the shove on the fist and the arm strength, so damage and knockback rise through real speed, not a hidden multiplier. Measured at close range: tap 6 damage, short hold 9, half hold 20, full hold 47 (the victim slides about 8 m on the heavy ones).
- 2026-10-04: Flow tweaks (owner asked for general smoothing): a jump pressed up to 6 frames early still happens, and one still works for 6 frames after walking off a ledge (coyote time); letting go of jump early cuts it short (tap = hop, hold = full 1.7 m); hits freeze the game 2-4 frames instead of 3-6; walking speed while charging 0.5 -> 0.65.
- 2026-10-04: Club hold loosened (owner: too much forced verticality, could not throw with speed). Found by measurement: the hold's angle barely changes how fast a club leaves the hand (14-22 m/s on a fast flick either way). The real limit is geometry: a rotating arm moves the hand at right angles to the arm, so a forward throw needs the arm roughly vertical at release (an overhand sweep), and that is exactly where the pose used to snap, because the fighter flips to face the other way as the cursor crosses straight up and the elbow bend flips with it. Fix: the guard bend now fades out as the cursor gets near straight up (longMelee.bendFade), so the arm and club straighten and the sweep over the top is smooth. Aiming straight down keeps the bend (a straight club would be driven into the floor, which a test caught). Also a less upright rest (guardElbow -1.0, guardWrist 0.3) and softer elbow/wrist springs so the club head lags and whips more.
- 2026-10-04: The charge is shown by the club cocking back gradually (owner), replacing the yellow glow: the pose blends from the guard to raised-and-leaning-back in step with the charge (measured: club angle -32 degrees at rest, -56 at half charge, -100 at full), and stays at that blend while flying forward after release.
- 2026-10-04: Charged throw (owner): right-click while holding the charge starts the swing (with half the usual lunge) and lets go of the club partway through the downswing, along the aim, with a boost that grows with the charge (throw.boost 11 m/s at full, on top of the arm's own speed). Measured: even a short hold throws cleanly forward at about 5 m/s, a half charge about 16, a full charge about 26 (before trimming the boost from 14 to 11). A plain right-click with no charge still just drops the club with the speed of your own swing and movement, so skilled players can build better throws by hand with mouse speed and body movement. The throw does damage by the same speed x weapon rule as any hit.
- 2026-10-04: DESIGN NOTE from the owner: the art will eventually be 2.5D, so not everything has to live on the one flat plane. The dodge's "background plane" is the first use of that (a separate collision plane that only touches the ground). Keep the simulation's plane idea general (a thing is on a plane, and planes decide what touches what) so later arena objects, projectiles and backgrounds can use it too.
- 2026-10-04: Local and online controls (owner): local multiplayer is gamepads; online co-op uses mouse aim or gamepads. A second keyboard layout is NOT being built. The dodge is the one evasive move (no dash or roll); wall slide and wall jump are fundamental mechanics. Weapon classes stay at long melee and short melee (punch-style) until Phase 4. No hit freeze (owner): big moments are shown with visual indicators, not by slowing other players.
- 2026-10-04: Phase 2 started on the owner's instruction ("progress through the next milestone"); the Phase 1 gate was treated as passed by the owner's continued iteration. A formal "friends ask for a rematch" check is the Phase 2 gate.
- 2026-10-04: Slice 1 of Phase 2. Punch knockback cut to 35% (fist.knockbackMul), the lunge into a punch halved, and damage restored through the fist damage factor (2.0 -> 3.0): tap 11 damage / half hold 18 / full hold 48, with the victim launched 4-6 m/s and sliding 0.8-2.1 m (it was up to 14 m/s and 8 m). Punch poses are smoother: elbow and wrist targets glide each frame (arm.poseSmooth 0.3) instead of snapping, and the throw and recovery last 10 and 12 frames.
- 2026-10-04: Hit freeze removed entirely (owner). A ring flashes where a big hit lands (indicator.minImpact 40) as the first visual indicator for big moments.
- 2026-10-04: Crouch (owner): hold S or stick-down / d-pad-down. The body really gets lower (capsule half-height 0.25 -> 0.12, head down 0.16 more, so swings aimed at the head can miss), walking is half speed, a jump from a full crouch goes 12% higher and a lunge or punch carries 30% more momentum. Only works on the ground.
- 2026-10-04: Dodge recovery (owner): you cannot attack from the background plane, and for 6 frames (0.1 s) after coming back, so a swing that missed you can be punished.
- 2026-10-04: Slice 2 of Phase 2: walls. A tall wall stands outside each end of the platform with a 1.3 m gap (arena.wallGap). A fighter knocked off an end falls into the gap, can slide down the wall by pushing toward it (wall slide, 1.5 m/s) and wall-jump back out (kicked away at 6.5 m/s, up at 10 m/s, steering off for 10 frames). Measured: jumping within about 0.25-0.3 s of touching the wall gets you back on the platform; waiting longer drops you too far. Knock-offs are therefore still deadly but not hopeless. Wall contact is detected from the body capsule and the head, because a leaning body touches a wall with its head first (a bug the first version had: the fighter ran into the wall and was never seen to touch it).
- 2026-10-04: Slice 3 of Phase 2: weapons. (1) Anyone can pick up any loose weapon within 1.6 m with right-click on empty hands (owner's Q2): the world picks the nearest loose club (not one dropped in the last 0.75 s), and the club changes owner and hands, including its damage credit and collision group. The dead can be looted at once. (2) Disarming (owner's Q3, preliminary values chosen by me, to be tuned in playtests): where a hit lands decides. A hit on the hand, or the grip end of the club, disarms at impact 28; elsewhere on the arm it needs 55; in a club-on-club clash it needs 40 AND the attacker's club must be 1.4x faster than the defender's, so a defender who holds their club in the way blocks a normal swing (no damage, no disarm), and only a clearly faster club wins the clash. The knocked-out club is flung away from the blow and the victim staggers briefly. A gentle tap never disarms (tested). (3) Arena weapon rules, set per arena (arena.weaponRule): 'start' = everyone starts armed; 'spots' = nobody starts armed and the clubs lie at fixed spots on the platform; 'sky' = nobody starts armed and clubs fall from above. (4) A club lost in the void returns from the sky after 2 s, so there are always weapons in play. (5) A dead fighter's floppy fists no longer hurt anyone (a club they threw still does).
- 2026-10-04: Bug fixed (owner report): right-click while holding left-click at full charge did not start the throw. Cause, reproduced in a real browser: browsers do not send a "button pressed" event for a second mouse button pressed while the first is held, only a move event with changed button flags, so the game never saw the right-click. The input layer now reads the state of all mouse buttons on every mouse event. (The simulation side was always right; a test now covers a charge held far past full.)
- 2026-10-04: Slice 4 of Phase 2: local multiplayer, rounds and score. Player 1 is keyboard and mouse (and the first gamepad too, so a lone gamepad works); players 2-4 are the other gamepads (owner: local play is gamepads; no second keyboard layout). Plugging in or unplugging a gamepad changes the player count live: alone you get the training dummy and respawns; with 2-4 players it is a real fight on the same platform with four spread-out start spots. Last fighter standing wins the round and scores a point (the plan's 1 point to the fight winner); dead fighters stay down until the round ends; a draw scores nobody; after 2.5 s the next round starts with the scores kept. Colours: red, blue, yellow, green for players 1-4; the training dummy is cream. A scoreboard and "X wins the round!" banner are plain text over the game. Pad layout: left stick moves, right stick aims, right trigger or bumper attacks, A jumps, B or left bumper dodges, stick down or d-pad down crouches, X drops or picks up a weapon.
- 2026-10-04: The training dummy now always holds a club (even when startArmed is off) so you can practise disarming. Its club never damages anyone while the dummy holds it (it has no AI, so a dangling club would only hurt players brushing past). If you knock it out, the club lies loose and you can take it; it is back in the dummy's hand when the dummy respawns.
- 2026-10-04: Crouch redone in the Stick Man style (owner: the old one 'crunched' the head). The body and head stay rigid and are no longer squashed; the hips sink (the collision capsule shortens from 0.25 to 0.06 half-height, so the head ducks about as low as before) and the drawn legs stay full length but splay wide to either side so the feet stay on the floor. Removed the headLift and legShorten settings; crouch.legSpread and crouch.minHalfHeight now drive it.
- 2026-10-04: Owner: crouch much faster (crouch.rate 0.25 -> 0.6) and walking about 10% faster (motion.moveSpeed 5.5 -> 6.05), for more dynamic weapon play. The faster crouch exposed a bug: the capsule shrinks so fast the feet briefly lose the floor and the game cancelled the crouch (flicker). Fixed with a short grace (crouch.airGrace, 14 frames) that does not apply while rising, so jumping still ends a crouch at once.
- 2026-10-04: Physics body rebuild (owner: crouch should feel like Stick Fight, all the way down to lying, by physics not animation stages). Fighters now have real legs (thigh + shin each, hip and knee motors reaching for a walk cycle) and are held up by an invisible stand spring: a ray from the hips finds the floor and pushes the body to the stand height. Holding down lowers that height smoothly and relaxes the leg motors, so the legs fold however they are bent; near the bottom the body lets go of its upright spring (keeping spin damping) and a gentle push tips it the way it already leans, so you end up lying down; you can crawl (35% speed); letting go stands you back up. A second arm was added (owner): pure decoration, motorless, touches only the floor and walls. Two fixes found while building it: leg motors aim relative to the body (aiming at world-down made a planted foot twist the body over), and the chest got extra turning inertia (the light chest was being spun by the arm motor faster than balance could hold it). The dummy now holds its club in a low guard facing the player and plants its feet. Old drawn legs and capsule-shrink crouch removed.
- 2026-10-04: Unarmed combat reworked (owner). A TAP on left-click (released within 8 frames, grab.holdFrames) throws a quick punch with no wind-up (punch.power 0.7). HOLDING it reaches the hand out and grabs the first part of another fighter it touches (a revolute joint between your fist and their part); holding on has no time limit (owner), swing the mouse to whirl them, let go of the button to fling them (their speed x grab.fling). Grabs only work unarmed. A hit on the grabber of impact 30 or more (grab.breakImpact), dying, dodging, or the victim dodging breaks the hold with no fling. A flung fighter is 'thrown' for 1.5 s: crashing into the floor, a wall, or a third fighter hurts them (and the third fighter) by crash speed x grab.slamFactor through the normal damage curve; a ring-out kills as always. The old wind-up/heavy punch was removed.
- 2026-10-04: Three additions (owner). (1) The second arm joins unarmed punches and grabs: it points along the aim, trailing the main arm slightly, and flops otherwise; still collides with nothing but floor and walls, so it never affects play (tuning.offHand). (2) Body collisions: a fighter moving at least 5 m/s and 1.6x faster than the fighter they touch hurts them by closing speed x 1.6 through the normal damage curve, with a shove (body.*). Landing on someone from above (contact along the vertical, attacker higher and falling) is a STOMP: factor 3.2 and a 'stomp' event, kept separate from 'hit' so the special animation can hook onto it later. A flung fighter's crashes use the slam rules instead (no double counting). Walking into each other is harmless (tested). (3) Hold W (gamepad: left stick up or d-pad up) in the air to spin forward the way you face (flip.spin 11 rad/s, a full flip fits in one jump), balance is off while holding; after landing or letting go, a tilted body is turned smoothly upright at a capped rate (flip.right*). W and Up Arrow no longer jump: jump is Space (and A on a pad). Flip works only in the air for now (holding it on the ground does nothing).
- 2026-10-04: Parry (owner): no new control, pure physics. When a club swing (attacker) hits a held club that is nearly still, the swing is parried: attacker's club must be at least 6 m/s and 3x faster than the blocking club, which must be under 2.5 m/s (parry.*). The swinger's club is sent back the way it came at 0.9x its speed (at least 7 m/s) with its spin reversed, the swinger's whole body is pushed back 3 m/s and staggers 14 frames, and cannot start another attack for 0.4 s (the window to counter); the blocker is untouched. Shown by a blue-white double ring, a small shake and a ring-of-steel sound, event 'parry'. A club that is itself swinging fast clashes as before (can disarm); a hit near the hand still disarms (the hand is the weak spot). Club contacts are now resolved before body contacts for each swing, so a parried swing cannot also hit the body in the same frame. Checked in a real swing: a blocker held their club up, the charge was parried, they lost no HP and kept the club. NOTE the numbers are first guesses: how still the blocking club must be (maxSpeed) decides how forgiving a block is.
- 2026-10-04: Grabs now have a natural limit (owner, replacing 'infinite hold'). A grab gives out after 2.5 s (grab.maxFrames 150): they drop out of your hands without a fling, and you must let go of the button and press again to regrab. Any hit on the grabber of impact 20 or more (grab.breakImpact, lowered from 30) breaks the hold: the held fighter can hit their way out, and so can a third fighter. Found and fixed: breaking on a hit only worked for crash and body damage; club and punch hits (hit()) never broke a grab. The intended flow: an unarmed fighter grabs someone to stop being hurt, tries to throw them off the map or away, then goes to get a weapon.
- 2026-10-04: Ponytail review of the session's diff: no unused tuning settings; cut a duplicate 'hit' event (an ordinary body slam announced itself twice: double sound and splat) and merged the offHand tuning section into offArm. - 2026-10-05: SLAM REWORK built. The steering is gone (no turning them head-down, no dragging them under you, no forced fast fall, no soft straight-down arm, no extra pull when swinging someone from the ground). Measured first: with the physics left completely alone a slam almost never happened (the grabbing arm cannot swing a body, so they just dangled and landed on their feet; 0 of 6 tries). So the slam is now a real arm move, a suplex: while you hold S in the air with someone in your hand, your grabbing arm gets stronger (slam.armMul 4) and sweeps from where it is up over your head toward your back (slam.sweepRate 6 rad/s, to slam.sweepTo, behind you and down); they go over in that arc through the grip and land behind you. Then the arm is back to its normal grip. It is a slam when any part of them (not their feet if they land upright) hits the ground at 4 m/s or more, measured at the spot that hits: damage as for a throw that hard (grab.slamFactor) times slam.bonus 1.3, times the head multiplier if they land on their head; they are knocked down and you let go. Measured over 30 slams (6 ways of jumping x 5 start spots): every one lands, usually about 20 damage, up to 45-60, no kill from a normal jump; head-first from 4 m up kills. Slower sweeps missed more, faster ones also missed more and varied wildly (9 rad/s: up to 112, a kill).
