# Balance lab: 4-player brawls

72 rounds of 4 bots (seeds 101, 102, 103), the real game: eras in order, weapons arriving, props on the maps. Took 0.7 minutes to run. Bots are steadier than people: read this for outliers, not as the last word on feel.

## The headlines

- **Round length:** 10 s on average (half the rounds are under 9 s). The target is about a minute.
- **How people die:** 20% knocked or fallen off the stage, 80% beaten down.
- **Rounds nobody won in 180 s** (stopped by the lab): 0 of 72.
- **Draws** (the last two went down together): 4.
