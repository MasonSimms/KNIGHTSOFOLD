# Balance lab: 4-player brawls

72 rounds of 4 bots (seeds 101, 102, 103), the real game: eras in order, weapons arriving, props on the maps. Took 1.5 minutes to run. Bots are steadier than people: read this for outliers, not as the last word on feel.

## The headlines

- **Round length:** 15 s on average (half the rounds are under 12 s). The target is about 20 s (owner, 2026-10-07).
- **How people die:** 50% knocked or fallen off the stage, 50% beaten down.
- **Rounds nobody won in 180 s** (stopped by the lab): 0 of 72.
